# Licensing decisions

No project license is selected by this starter. Before publishing, choose and add
appropriate license text for the manuscript, software, experiment configurations,
and figures. Record third-party content and attribution obligations separately.
Do not label the project MIT, Apache, CC BY, or another license without an actual
maintainer decision and matching LICENSE text.

The three repositories may have different licenses. Confirm compatibility and
redistribution rights before adding submodules or externally sourced figures.
This checklist is a setup reminder, not legal advice or a license grant.
